Dream review

Keys: A accept, E edit, R reject, U undo, N/P next and previous.

One dream at a time. Choosing a decision saves it and stays on the same dream; move on with Next. An edit accepts the dream with your wording, and the drafted wording is kept beside it. Decisions are kept in this browser until you download them. Save the file as reviews/dreams-decisions.json in the run and give it to dreams-ingest.
