Fix review

Keys: A accept, E edit, R reject, U undo, L choose a dream to link, N/P next and previous.

One fix at a time, in the judges’ ranking order, with unlinked fixes last. Each fix appears once, with every dream it’s linked to. Remove a link with its Remove button, or add one to any accepted dream. Your links may take a dream past the assembler’s limit of five. Choosing a decision saves it and stays on the same fix; move on with Next. An edit accepts the fix with your wording, and the drafted wording is kept beside it. Decisions and links are kept in this browser until you download them. Save the file as reviews/fixes-decisions.json in the run and give it to fixes-ingest.
