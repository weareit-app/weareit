## Fixes

### Fix 1: Ask Mom how long she can keep going

- **Beginning:** she raises the question with Mom.
- **Completion (intended result):** she knows how long Mom can keep doing Tuesdays, and the answer is recorded.
- **Downstream benefit:** she can plan around it.
- **May help toward:** Stop worrying; Keep working.
- **Needs to start:** none.
- **Needs to succeed:** none besides the result.
- **Made of:** one step, talk to Mom. This is a checklist.
- **Takes part:** her (asks); Mom (answers).
- **Outcomes:**
  - Mom can continue for a stated period (pending, said). It establishes Tuesday coverage until that date and no more.
  - Mom says she can't keep going (not hoped for, inferred). It makes "Mom looks after him on Tuesdays" become no from that date. Say in a sentence that it changed.
  - Mom says she'll keep going with no end date (hoped for, inferred). It establishes Tuesdays as covered with no stated end.
  - Mom hasn't answered yet (pending, inferred). It establishes nothing.

### Fix 2: Apply for the childcare subsidy

- **Beginning:** she starts checking eligibility.
- **Completion (intended result):** a complete application has been submitted. This is not "approved", which is an outcome.
- **Downstream benefit:** lower cost, may help toward Care I can afford.
- **May help toward:** Care I can afford; Stop worrying; Keep working.
- **Needs to start:** none. Checking eligibility is itself the first step.
- **Needs to succeed:** an employer letter (heard) and proof of hours (heard).
- **Made of:** (1) check eligibility, (2) get the employer letter, (3) get proof of hours, (4) submit. These are steps toward, not a checklist. After submitting, she still has to record the decision, and eligibility might fail.
- **Takes part:** her; her employer (letter and hours proof); the subsidy office (decides).
- **Outcomes:**
  - Application accepted as complete and approved (hoped for, inferred). It establishes that she gets a subsidy, but not the amount or that the cheaper daycare is covered.
  - Not eligible (not hoped for, inferred). It establishes that this route is closed.
  - Decision pending (pending, inferred). It establishes nothing.

### Fix 3: Ask the manager to approve a later start two days a week

- **Beginning:** she makes the request to her manager.
- **Completion (intended result):** the manager's decision on the later start is recorded.
- **Downstream benefit:** she can use the cheaper place that opens at eight.
- **May help toward:** Keep working; Care I can afford.
- **Needs to start:** none.
- **Needs to succeed:** none besides the result. The manager's willingness is an outcome, not a precondition.
- **Made of:** (1) make the request, (2) follow up on the decision. This is a checklist, since the result is just the decision.
- **Takes part:** her (asks); her manager (decides).
- **Outcomes:**
  - Later start agreed for both days (hoped for, inferred). It establishes a later start on both days.
  - Agreed for one of the two days only (partial outcome, hoped for, inferred). It establishes a later start on that day, and says nothing about the other.
  - Declined (not hoped for, inferred). It establishes no later start.
  - Manager "thinking about it" (pending, said). It establishes nothing.
  - Manager reverses an earlier agreement (not hoped for, inferred). It withdraws the later-start outcome from this fix. If Fix 4 already started, a new outcome on Fix 4 is needed as well, because the graph won't update it.

### Fix 4: Enroll him in the cheaper place that opens at eight

- **Beginning:** she contacts the place about a spot.
- **Completion (intended result):** he is enrolled and attending on the days she needs.
- **Downstream benefit:** lower cost and more reliable care.
- **May help toward:** Care I can afford; Stop worrying; He is somewhere he's happy.
- **Needs to start:** none.
- **Needs to succeed:** Fix 3: later start agreed (inferred), since the place opens at eight. It also needs a space at the place and a price she can afford (both not checked).
- **Made of:** (1) ask about space and price, (2) enroll, (3) start attending. These are steps toward, since the result also depends on the place having space.
- **Takes part:** her; the cheaper daycare; her manager (via Fix 3).
- **Outcomes:**
  - Enrolled, with space and an affordable price (hoped for, inferred). It establishes care for the days covered by the later start.
  - No space, or too expensive (not hoped for, inferred). It establishes that this option is closed.
  - Waiting on Fix 3 (pending, inferred). It establishes nothing.

### Fix 5: Join the babysitting swap group

- **Outcomes:**
  - Joined (hoped for, inferred). It establishes membership.
  - Not accepted, or the group is full (not hoped for, inferred). It establishes no membership.
  - Waiting to hear (pending, inferred). It establishes nothing.

## Questions

- **Made of (steps toward) versus checklist.** I couldn't always tell whether a fix is complete when its steps are done. Fix 3 is a checklist only if the result is the decision rather than the start. I chose the decision.
- **Fix 5's boundary.** I chose joining, not regular care, because the guide says joined is narrower than regular care. If regular care is what she wants, it would be a separate fix. I don't know how to decide that without asking.
- **Outcome kinds.** "Pending" feels like an outcome kind and also like the absence of an outcome. I recorded it as an outcome that establishes nothing.
- **Whether outcomes may repeat.** The guide doesn't say whether the same fix can have many outcomes over time. I assumed yes.
- **Sources on inferred facts.** For "has not joined the group" I marked no/inferred, but the notes never say. It may be better as not checked. I couldn't decide whether silence plus the context counts as "no".
- **Dreams.** Whether "Care I can afford" is too close to the pain itself, so it may be too broad.

## Effort

The hard part was keeping each fix to a single activity. Subsidy, daycare, and swap group each wanted to be both "get it" and "use it". The ambiguous parts were whether the later start is a precondition for Fix 4 (I made it "needs to succeed", pointing at Fix 3) and how to represent a manager who is still undecided. I worked from the packet alone, and it took about 15 minutes of reasoning.
