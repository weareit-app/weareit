## Fixes

### Fix 1: Find out whether I qualify for a childcare subsidy
- **Beginning:** looking up the subsidy rules or contacting the office that runs it.
- **Completion (intended result):** I know whether I qualify.
- **Downstream benefit:** knowing whether applying (Fix 2) is worth it.
- **May help toward:** Care that doesn't eat my pay; Childcare I don't have to worry about.
- **Needs to start:** none.
- **Needs to succeed:** none besides the result.
- **Made of:** find the rules for my area; compare my income and hours to them; ask the office if unclear. **Checklist** (once done, I know).
- **Who takes part:** me (checks); subsidy office (gives information).
- **Outcomes:**
  - Likely eligible: hoped for; source said-once-recorded (none yet). Establishes F25 = yes.
  - Not eligible: not hoped for. Establishes F25 = no.
  - Pending: current state (said: never checked).

### Fix 2: Get a childcare subsidy approved
- **Beginning:** starting the application.
- **Completion (intended result):** a subsidy is approved.
- **Downstream benefit:** a lower childcare cost, possibly making the daycare with space (F17, F18) affordable.
- **May help toward:** Care that doesn't eat my pay; Keep working; Childcare I don't have to worry about.
- **Needs to start:** none (Fix 1 helps but someone could apply without it).
- **Needs to succeed:** being eligible (Fix 1: likely eligible); an employer letter (heard); proof of hours (heard).
- **Made of:** get the employer letter from my manager; gather proof of hours; submit the application; wait for the decision. **Steps toward** (the decision still has to be recorded).
- **Who takes part:** me (applicant); manager or employer (writes the letter); subsidy office (decides).
- **Outcomes:**
  - Approved: hoped for. Establishes "subsidy in place: yes", with the amount.
  - Refused: not hoped for. Establishes "subsidy in place: no".
  - Pending: not started.

## Questions

- **Checklist or steps toward for an application.** If the intended result is "a decision received", the steps pass the checklist test but you still record approved or refused. I made the result "approved" and called it steps toward; the guide doesn't say which framing to prefer.
- **An action already begun.** The manager has been asked and is thinking. I treated that as Fix 3 with a pending outcome rather than a fact, but F21 also records "no (undecided)". It's unclear whether both should exist.
- **Per-day facts when working days are unknown.** The guide asks for one fact per day, but I don't know which days are workdays, so I listed every day as not checked plus F8. That's noisy.
- **"Not hoped for" outcomes that end a dependency.** In Fix 7, failing to agree rules withdraws Fix 6's outcome? Arguably the family's agreement in principle still stands. The guide's withdraw test ("if this ends, what goes away") left this ambiguous.
- **Source mark for a pending outcome I've invented.** Outcomes like "approved" are possibilities, not things from the notes. The source marks fit facts better than hypothetical outcomes, so I only marked sources on outcomes the notes actually report.
- **"Care that doesn't eat my pay" as a dream.** It's close to restating the pain. I kept it because it's a real option she wants, but it may count as too close to a means.
- **Heard vs said for a friend's report.** "A friend told me about a group" — the group's existence is secondhand, but her being told is said. I marked heard.

## Effort

The hardest part was holding each fix to one activity: the subsidy, the cheaper place, and the nanny share each wanted to split into two or three fixes, and choosing where to cut (eligibility vs approval; find a family vs run a share) felt like judgement rather than a rule. The per-day fact rule produced a lot of "not checked" rows because the schedule is mostly unknown. Telling "needs to start" from "is one of the steps" was easy once applied, but "needs to succeed" overlapped with steps (the employer letter is both a step and a need). Withdrawals across fixes (manager reversing affecting Fix 4) were clear thanks to the stated limit. Roughly 35–45 minutes of focused work.
