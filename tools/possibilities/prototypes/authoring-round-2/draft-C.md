### Fix 1: Apply for the childcare subsidy

- **Beginning:** looking up the subsidy's eligibility rules. **Completion:** a complete application is submitted. **Downstream benefit:** approval, which could make the daycare with space (or any paid option) affordable.
- **May help toward:** Keep working; Weekly peace of mind.
- **Needs to start:** none (she can read the rules with nothing in hand). **Needs to succeed:** employer letter in hand (heard); proof of hours in hand (heard); eligibility rules allow her to apply (not checked).
- **Made of:** read the eligibility rules; ask the manager for the employer letter; gather proof of hours; fill in and submit. **Checklist:** yes, all four done means it's submitted.
- **Who takes part:** parent (applicant); manager (writes the letter); subsidy office (receives).
- **Outcomes:**
  - Application submitted — hoped for (inferred). Establishes "application submitted: yes".
  - Ineligible on reading the rules — not hoped for (inferred). Establishes "qualifies for subsidy: no". Withdraws nothing.
  - Manager declines to write the letter — not hoped for (inferred). Establishes "employer letter: no".
  - Waiting for the letter or documents — pending (inferred). Establishes nothing yet.

## Questions

- Splitting "apply" (Fix 1) from "get the decision" (Fix 2): the guide separates them as activities, but the decision isn't something she does. I wasn't sure whether to make it a fix or just an outcome of Fix 1 beyond completion.
- Source mark for "not checked" facts: the guide gives a source for facts, but a fact the notes don't mention has no source except my noticing the silence. I marked these "inferred".
- Weekday facts: the guide asks for one fact per day, but only Tuesday is named. I listed every weekday as "not checked", which may be noise.
- Fix 3 has already begun (the manager was asked). I treated "she'd think about it" as a pending outcome of a live fix rather than a fact; it could also be only the fact "manager considering: yes".
- "Partial outcome" and "kind": a one-day agreement is both hoped for and not. I wrote "hoped for in part", which isn't one of the three kinds.
- Whether "Keep working" is too broad a dream (it would fit many pains). I kept it because the notes name it directly.

## Effort

The hardest part was holding each fix to one activity: the subsidy naturally reads as "apply and get approved", the daycare as "enrol and attend", and the guide forces a choice each time, which then forces a second fix or a downstream note. Ambiguities clustered around what to do with silence (unknown workdays, unknown fees, unknown waiting-list status) and how to mark a source for things the notes never mention. Partial outcomes with a single "kind" also didn't quite fit. Writing it took roughly 25–30 minutes of thinking and drafting.
