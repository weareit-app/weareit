## Fixes

### 1. Get approved for the childcare subsidy
- Intended result: she is approved for the subsidy. This is not "apply", and it is not "get money paid to a provider". (assumed choice between those results)
- May help toward: dream 4 (affordable); dream 2 (keep working) indirectly. (assumed)
- Needs to start: nothing known. She could begin by checking eligibility without anything else. (assumed)
- Needs to succeed: she meets the eligibility rules (needs checking); an employer letter (stated as heard, needs checking); proof of hours (stated as heard, needs checking). None of these establishes approval. The decision still has to be observed.
- Made of: steps toward, not a checklist. Check the rules, ask the employer for a letter, gather proof of hours, submit. After these she still has to record what the agency decided. (assumed steps; the first three are stated as things she heard)
- Takes part: her (applicant); employer/manager (supplies the letter) (assumed); the subsidy agency (decides) (assumed).
- Outcomes:
  - Approved (hoped, pending). Establishes only that she is approved under that scheme. It does not establish that any provider has space or that the cost is manageable. (assumed)
  - Denied (unhoped, pending). Establishes that this application was refused. It does not by itself rule out reapplying. (assumed)
  - Withdraws: if approval later ends (income or hours change, or the scheme ends), it withdraws "approved under that scheme" from that source only. Other fixes stay as they were. (assumed)

### 2. Manager agrees to a later start two days a week
- Intended result: her manager agrees, in a form she can rely on, that she may start later on two days a week. "Think about it" doesn't count as agreement. (assumed)
- May help toward: dreams 2 and 4, by making fix 3 possible. (assumed)
- Needs to start: nothing. She can ask or follow up now. (assumed)
- Needs to succeed: the manager's agreement. The notes don't say whether it also needs approval above the manager (needs checking).
- Made of: a single step, so the checklist question doesn't apply. She still has to record what was said and whether it is permanent. (assumed)
- Takes part: her; manager (decides) (stated).
- Outcomes:
  - Agreed (hoped, pending). Establishes that she may start later on two days. It does not establish which two days, or for how long. (assumed)
  - Declined (unhoped, pending). Fix 3 loses its schedule support. (assumed)
  - Withdraws: if the manager later reverses, it withdraws the later start. Whatever she has already arranged at the cheaper place is not undone automatically. (assumed)

### 3. Use the cheaper place that opens at eight, two days a week
- Intended result: he is in the cheaper place on the two later-start days. (assumed)
- May help toward: dreams 1, 2, and 4. Dream 3 is unknown (needs checking; nothing is said about whether he'd be happy there).
- Needs to start: nothing known. She could ask about space first. (assumed)
- Needs to succeed: fix 2 agreed (stated); a space for him (needs checking); a cost she can manage (needs checking). The place being cheaper doesn't make it affordable.
- Made of: steps toward, not a checklist: ask about space and price, visit, enrol, begin attending. After these she still has to see whether it works. (assumed)
- Takes part: her; the cheaper place's staff (assumed); her manager (indirect, via fix 2).
- Outcomes:
  - Space and price are workable (hoped, pending). Establishes only that she can enrol. It does not establish that he is happy there. (assumed)
  - No space (unhoped, pending).
  - Withdraws: if the place closes or the manager reverses fix 2, it withdraws the two-day arrangement. Her mom's Tuesdays stay as they are. (assumed)

### 4. Join the babysitting swap
- Intended result: she is in the swap and gets hours of care for him, in exchange for hours she gives. (assumed)
- May help toward: dreams 1 and 4 (it costs hours, not money). Dream 2 only if the hours fit her shifts (needs checking).
- Needs to start: being let into the group (needs checking; the notes say she'd have to find time to do her share, not that she has to be accepted).
- Needs to succeed: time to do her share (needs checking); other members available when she works early shifts (needs checking).
- Made of: steps toward, not a checklist: ask her friend, join, earn hours, use them. After these she still has to see whether the hours covered what she needed. (assumed)
- Takes part: her; her friend (introduces) (stated); other parents in the group (assumed).
- Outcomes:
  - Joined (hoped, pending). Establishes that she is a member. It does not establish any hours.
  - Hours earned (hoped, pending). Establishes a balance she can spend. It does not establish that anyone is available when she needs them. (assumed)
  - Can't make time for her share (unhoped, pending).
  - Withdraws: if she leaves or is removed, it withdraws her membership and any unspent hours. Hours already used stay used. (assumed)

### 5. Share a nanny with a second family
- Intended result: a shared-nanny arrangement is running for him and one other child. (assumed)
- May help toward: dreams 1 to 4 (reliability and cost are shared); dream 3 depends on the nanny (needs checking).
- Needs to start: a family nearby with a child about his age (stated); the two families agree on the rules (stated).
- Needs to succeed: a nanny both families accept (needs checking; the notes never mention finding one); a cost she can manage (needs checking); the two families' hours matching (needs checking). A signed agreement would not establish that it works.
- Made of: steps toward, not a checklist: find a family, agree rules, find a nanny, start. After these she still has to see whether it works. (assumed)
- Takes part: her; the other family (stated as needed); a nanny (assumed).
- Outcomes:
  - Family found (hoped, pending). Establishes only that a partner exists.
  - Rules agreed (hoped, pending). Establishes that both agreed to those terms.
  - Nanny found (hoped, pending).
  - No family found or no agreement (unhoped, pending).
  - Withdraws: if the other family leaves, it withdraws the sharing arrangement and the cost split. It does not withdraw a nanny's relationship with her. (needs checking)

## Questions

- **Mom's Tuesdays.** Is this a fix, a fact, or both? I treated it as a fact (yes) plus a risk (can she continue?). The guide has no obvious place for "an arrangement that exists and may end." Withdraws seems to apply, but no fix entry fits.
- **Fix 1: "apply" or "get approved"?** The guide asks for the intended result, but the notes don't say. I picked "approved" and left "applied" as a step.
- **Needs to start or needs to succeed for the employer letter and proof of hours?** I put them under succeed because she could start without them. They may be needed to submit.
- **Fix 2 as a fix.** It is one step with one outcome, and it works more like a dependency of fix 3. The guide doesn't say whether a fix may exist only to support another fix, or how to show that dependency between fixes.
- **Dream 4.** It is close to the pain restated and may be too broad. I kept it marked assumed.
- **Dream 3 across fixes.** The notes say nothing about whether he would be happy in any of these, so I haven't linked it to anything.
- **Whether a nanny is part of fix 5.** The notes imply one but never mention hiring.

## Effort

About 20 minutes of thinking, and the writing took longer than the thinking. The hardest part was deciding what counts as one fact (for example, "cheaper place has space" and "is affordable" were easy to merge). The next hardest was separating the intended result from the process, especially for the subsidy and the nanny. The guide is ambiguous in four places. First, whether "needs to start" can include a condition that only enables the first step. Second, whether an outcome that is a hoped-for state can also be a fact later. Third, how to represent one fix supporting another. Fourth, whether "stated" applies to things the author heard second-hand, such as the employer letter and the swap group. I used "stated" for what the notes say, even when the notes themselves are repeating hearsay, and flagged that.
