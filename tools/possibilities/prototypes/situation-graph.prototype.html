Situation graph

Question: if It records dreams, fixes, a few facts about an imagined person's situation, and three different relationships between them, can it answer questions nobody wrote the answers to? The three relationships are kept apart: a fix is made of smaller steps (all of them done means it's done), a fix needs something that a fact or another fix can provide, and a fix may help toward a dream, with no promise. Every answer below comes from the same few general rules over the graph, so editing the graph (left, "Change the graph") changes the answers by itself. Facts never remove a dream; they change how relevant it is or how a fix fits. "No known way yet" is a gap to fill, not the same as "not possible here".
