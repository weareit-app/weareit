Situation graph

Question: can a small graph of dreams, fixes, and facts about an imagined person give trustworthy answers that nobody wrote by hand? Version 2 narrows every claim. Doing something isn't the same as it working: each fix has possible outcomes, and only an outcome provides anything. Not known isn't false: facts are yes, no, or not checked. "Made of" completes a larger step only when it's truly a checklist. A fix may help toward a dream; nothing here ever says a dream is reached, and "blocked" only ever means every approach modeled here. The relationships stay distinct: made of, needs and provides, may help toward. Edit the graph and the same rules give new answers.
