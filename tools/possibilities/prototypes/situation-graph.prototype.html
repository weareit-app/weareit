Situation graph

Question: can explicit relationships between dreams, fixes, and facts about an imagined person produce dependency explanations, and keep them consistent as information changes, without anyone writing each answer by hand? What it shows so far: yes for explaining what's missing and what changed. What it doesn't show: that these explanations improve people's decisions enough to justify authoring and maintaining the graph, or anything about complete paths, lasting freedoms, or whether several actions fit together.

Rules in this version: any outcome can establish something, and whether it was hoped for is separate. A fix needs some things to start and may need others to succeed: those are necessary, never enough on their own. History is kept, and withdrawing something removes only what that source provided. Help is recorded per dream, not per fix. Facts are yes, no, or not checked. Nothing here says a dream is reached.
