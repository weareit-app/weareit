# Schedule brief: a commitment that repeats on the calendar

Read `briefs/kinds/_shared.md` first. This brief covers only Schedule fixes (`kind: schedule`, origin `kind:schedule`).

A Schedule is something that repeats at a set time: a weekly call with a friend, a monthly repair night, the same class every Tuesday. A date or a time triggers it, not something the person already does. Its title says what repeats and how often.

## Method

Three methods shape a Schedule:

- **A plan tied to a time** (Peter Gollwitzer's implementation intentions). "Every Sunday at 4pm, I call…" is followed far more often than "I'll call more". A Schedule names the slot.
- **Commit to someone** (the "Social" part of the Behavioural Insights Team's EAST framework: make it Easy, Attractive, Social, and Timely). A standing time with another person is harder to skip than one you keep alone. Look for a Schedule that someone else is part of.
- **Plan for the obstacle** (Gabriele Oettingen's WOOP: Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, and Plan). Name the most likely reason it would lapse and the fallback. If the fallback changes what the reader would do, it can be the copy's one plain limit ("If one of you can't make it, move it to the next evening").

For each accepted dream with a Schedule ceiling above zero, list what could repeat on the calendar to move a person toward it. Keep only the strongest, up to the ceiling.

## Method fields

Fill these in `method` before writing any copy:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `what` | What repeats ("A phone call with one old friend.") |
| `cadence` | How often ("Every week.", "On the first Saturday of each month.") |

You may also add `withWhom`, `slot` (a suggested day or time), `likelyObstacle`, and `ifThen` (the fallback). None is required, but the obstacle and the fallback often make the text more useful.

The first step is usually putting the first one on the calendar or inviting the other person. The method fields never appear as an "If…, then I will…" sentence in the copy.

## Is it really a Schedule?

| If… | then it's… |
| --- | --- |
| something the person already does triggers it, not a date or time | a Habit |
| it happens once | a Task |
| the point is getting better at a skill each time | a Practice |
| it's the regular meeting of a group the person joins or starts | a Group or New group. The kind follows the group, not the meeting time. |
| it has an end, with several steps toward a finished thing | a Project |

A Schedule's status is usually `real_now`.

## Examples

Good:
- **Title:** "Set up a standing Sunday call with one old friend"
- **Text:** "A fixed time takes away the 'we should catch up' back-and-forth. If one of you can't make it, move it to the next evening rather than skipping the week."
- **Method:** `what` "A phone call with one old friend.", `cadence` "Every Sunday.", `slot` "Sunday at 4pm", `likelyObstacle` "One of you has plans.", `ifThen` "Move it to Monday evening."

Bad:
- **Title:** "Stay in touch more"

There's no cadence, no slot, and no person. This is a wish, not a commitment.
