# Switch brief: replace something with a better version (trial)

Read `briefs/kinds/_shared.md` first. This brief covers only Switch fixes (`kind: switch`, origin `kind:switch`).

Switch is a trial kind. Only a pilot run (`"pilot": true` in `run.json`) plans and drafts it, and the pilot decides whether it stays.

A Switch fix replaces something with a better version of the same thing: a bank for a credit union, a phone plan for a cheaper one, one doctor for another. Its title names what the person switches to, and from what when it helps.

## Method

A Switch is Hirschman's exit with somewhere to go. A good Switch says what to switch to, why it's better for this dream, and what the move costs, such as fees, time, or features lost. Switching usually takes a few steps, so the `firstStep` is the first one that takes 20 minutes or less, such as comparing two options or opening the new account.

For each accepted dream with a Switch ceiling above zero, list what a person uses that has a better version, by the examples in the run. Keep only the strongest, up to the ceiling.

## Method fields

Fill these in `method` before writing any copy:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `switchFrom` | What the person replaces ("a bank that charges overdraft fees") |
| `switchTo` | The better version of the same thing ("a credit union with no overdraft fees") |
| `tradeOff` | What the move costs or loses, said plainly ("Fewer branches, and a week of moving automatic payments.") |

## Is it really Switch?

| If… | then it's… |
| --- | --- |
| nothing replaces what the person stops using | Leave |
| the better version needs someone's yes, such as moving to another team | an Ask |
| it's a one-time change to a setting of something they keep using | a Task |
| the replacement is a service people qualify for, such as a free clinic | Use a resource |

A Switch fix's status is usually `real_now`, or `existing_resource` when it moves the person to a service they qualify for. Every name and number in the copy must appear in the cited exemplar's record. A speculative Switch names the type of thing to switch to ("a credit union"), never a company.

## Examples

Good:
- **Title:** "Move your checking account to a credit union"
- **Text:** "Many credit unions charge lower fees than big banks, and some charge no overdraft fees at all. Moving takes about a week of updating automatic payments."
- **Method:** `switchFrom` "a bank that charges overdraft fees", `switchTo` "a credit union with no overdraft fees", `tradeOff` "Fewer branches, and a week of moving automatic payments."
- **Lens and ambition:** `market`, `this-year`.

Bad:
- **Title:** "Find a better bank"

This one names no better version and no reason it's better, and says nothing about the cost of moving.
