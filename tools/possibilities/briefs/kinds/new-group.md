# New group brief: start a group that doesn't exist yet

Read `briefs/kinds/_shared.md` first. This brief covers only New group fixes (`kind: new-group`, origin `kind:new-group`).

A New group fix is starting a group that doesn't exist yet: a tenant association in your building, a lunch group at work, a parents' group for one school. Its title names what the reader would start.

## Method

Three methods shape a New group:

- **Check what exists first** (asset-based community development, from John Kretzmann and John McKnight). If a group the reader could join usually already exists where they are, that's a Group fix, and usually a better one. Write a New group only where starting one makes sense.
- **The first real test** (Jane McAlevey's structure tests). A group shows it's real with a first public action most members take part in, like getting most of the building to sign one letter. Name that first test, not just "start a group".
- **Mutual aid, not charity** (Dean Spade). A group that meets a need directly gets people active and builds new relationships. Avoid copying charity models, where some give and others receive.

For each accepted dream with a New group ceiling above zero, ask who would gather, around what, and what they'd do first. Keep only the strongest, up to the ceiling.

## Method fields

Fill these in `method` before writing any copy:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `whoFor` | The people it's for ("The tenants in your building.") |
| `whatMembersDo` | What members would do together ("Raise repair problems together and negotiate with the landlord as one.") |
| `firstTest` | The first thing the group does together that shows it's working ("Most of the building signs one letter about the heat.") |
| `existingFirst` | What to check exists before starting one ("Whether the building already has a tenant association.") |

The first step is usually small: knocking on a few doors or asking two people about one shared problem.

## Is it really a New group?

| If… | then it's… |
| --- | --- |
| a group like it already exists where the reader is (the building already has a tenant association) | a Group |
| it ends once the first event is over | a Project. The kind follows what lasts. |
| it's a regular time with one or two people | a Schedule |
| it gathers people behind one demand to a decision-maker | an Ask, not a New group, unless the group lasts beyond the demand. Leave the Ask out. |
| it's a tool It would build to gather people | a Feature |

A New group's status is usually `real_now`.

## Examples

Good:
- **Title:** "Start a tenant association in your building"
- **Text:** "Start with a knock on five doors about one shared problem, like the broken heat. If most of the building will sign one letter to the landlord, you have a group."
- **Method:** `whoFor` "The tenants in your building.", `whatMembersDo` "Raise repairs together and negotiate with the landlord as one.", `firstTest` "Most of the building signs one letter about the heat.", `existingFirst` "Whether the building already has a tenant association."

Bad:
- **Title:** "Start a community group"

There's no one it's for, nothing members do, and no first test.
