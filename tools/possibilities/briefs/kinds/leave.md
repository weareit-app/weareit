# Leave brief: stop being part of something (trial)

Read `briefs/kinds/_shared.md` first. This brief covers only Leave fixes (`kind: leave`, origin `kind:leave`).

Leave is a trial kind. Only a pilot run (`"pilot": true` in `run.json`) plans and drafts it, and the pilot decides whether it stays.

A Leave fix is stopping being part of something, with nothing lined up to replace it: a group chat that keeps work going at night, a subscription, a committee, a friendship that only drains. Its title names what the person leaves.

## Method

Hirschman separates exit (leaving) from voice (asking for change from inside). Leave is exit. It earns a fix only when leaving is the better move: when asking for change has failed or can't work, and what the person loses is smaller than what they get back. Say the trade-off plainly. A Leave fix that hides the cost isn't honest.

For each accepted dream with a Leave ceiling above zero, list what a person could stop being part of that stands between them and the dream. Keep only the strongest, up to the ceiling. Most dreams get none.

## Method fields

Fill these in `method` before writing any copy:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `leaving` | What the person stops being part of ("the after-hours work chat") |
| `tradeOff` | What they give up by leaving, said plainly ("You may hear about changes a day later.") |

## Is it really Leave?

| If… | then it's… |
| --- | --- |
| the person replaces it with a better version of the same thing | Switch |
| the person asks for it to change instead | an Ask |
| it's a one-time change to their own setting, not leaving something they belong to | a Task |

A Leave fix's status is usually `real_now`. Never tell someone to leave a job, a home, or a relationship as if it were simple. Those are big decisions, and the copy says what they'd lose.

## Examples

Good:
- **Title:** "Leave the work chat on your phone"
- **Text:** "If work messages can only reach you on the laptop, the evening stays yours. You may hear about some changes the next morning, and most can wait that long."
- **Method:** `leaving` "the work chat app on the phone", `tradeOff` "Messages sent after hours wait until the morning."

Bad:
- **Title:** "Cut toxic things out of your life"

This one names nothing to leave, and "toxic" is a judgment, not an action.
