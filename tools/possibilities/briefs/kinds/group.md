# Group brief: join a group that already exists

Read `briefs/kinds/_shared.md` first. This brief covers only Group fixes (`kind: group`, origin `kind:group`).

A Group fix is joining a kind of group that already exists in most places: a building's tenant association, a union, a repair café, a parents' group at school. Its title names the group.

## Method

Two methods shape a Group:

- **Start from what's already there** (asset-based community development, from John Kretzmann and John McKnight). Communities already have associations: congregations, PTAs, tenant groups, unions, clubs. Look for one the reader can join before anyone suggests starting one.
- **Say what members do** (Marshall Ganz's organizing practices: relationships, story, strategy, action, and structure). A group is worth joining for what its members actually do together. Name that, not just that the group exists.

For each accepted dream with a Group ceiling above zero, list kinds of groups that exist in most places and would move a person toward it. Keep only the strongest, up to the ceiling.

## Method fields

Fill these in `method` before writing any copy:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `whatMembersDo` | What members actually do together ("Compare notes on repairs and rent increases, and sometimes negotiate as one.") |
| `howToFind` | How the reader finds theirs ("Your city's tenant union usually keeps a list of building groups.") |

The first step is usually going to one meeting or messaging one organizer.

## Is it really a Group?

| If… | then it's… |
| --- | --- |
| it doesn't exist yet and the reader would start it | a New group |
| it has an end | a Project |
| it's a regular time with one or two people, not a group with members | a Schedule |
| the reader goes to it for a service, not to take part (a clinic, a hotline) | Use a resource. Leave it out. |
| it's a match or a directory It would build to find people | a Feature |

A Group's status is usually `real_now` or `existing_resource`.

## Examples

Good:
- **Title:** "Join your building's tenant association"
- **Text:** "Members compare notes on repairs and rent increases and sometimes negotiate as one. Your city's tenant union usually keeps a list of building groups."
- **Method:** `whatMembersDo` "Compare notes on repairs and rent, and sometimes negotiate with the landlord as one.", `howToFind` "The city's tenant union keeps a list of building groups."

Bad:
- **Title:** "Connect with your community"

There's no group to join and nothing members do.
