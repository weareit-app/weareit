# Ask brief: something that needs someone else's yes

Read `briefs/kinds/_shared.md` first. This brief covers only Ask fixes (`kind: ask`, origin `kind:ask`).

An Ask is a request to one person or body with the power to say yes: a landlord, a manager, a school board, a city council member, or someone the person knows. The outcome depends on their answer, not on the person's own effort. Its title names who is asked and what for.

## Method

Two methods shape an Ask:

- **A named target and one demand** (the Midwest Academy strategy chart, and the Community Tool Box's action plans). The target is someone with the power to say yes, never a vague institution ("the government", "society"). The request is specific enough to answer yes or no.
- **Voice, not exit** (Hirschman). An Ask pushes for a change from inside something the person stays part of. Leaving it is a different kind of fix.

People underestimate how often others say yes to a direct request, by as much as half in Flynn and Lake's studies. A good Ask makes the request small and clear enough to say yes to.

For each accepted dream with an Ask ceiling above zero, list who could say yes to something that moves a person toward it, and what exactly they could say yes to. Prefer requests with a precedent in the run's examples. Keep only the strongest, up to the ceiling.

## Method fields

Fill these in `method` before writing any copy:

| Field | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| `decisionMaker` | Who can say yes, as a role a reader can find ("your landlord", "your city council member", "your manager") |
| `request` | The one specific request they can answer yes or no ("Move the weekly meeting before 4 p.m.") |

## Is it really an Ask?

| If… | then it's… |
| --- | --- |
| the person can do it alone, with no one's yes | a Task |
| the action is contacting a service that already exists for people like them (a hotline, a clinic, a benefits office) | Use a resource |
| it takes a campaign of several steps over weeks, with an end | a Project, or a Group or New group if it needs others to keep going |
| the person stops being part of something instead of asking it to change | Leave or Switch (pilot runs only) |
| it's something It would build, or a question It should ask the person | a Feature |

An Ask never implies the decision-maker has agreed or will agree. Its status is usually `real_now`, since anyone can ask today, or `societal_proposal` when the request is for a policy that doesn't exist yet. It is never `proposed_it_feature`. The `firstStep` is often a Task: writing the request down, finding who to send it to, or booking the meeting.

## Examples

Good:
- **Title:** "Ask your school board for a later start time"
- **Text:** "Some districts already start high school at 8:30 or later, and boards change start times when parents ask at a public meeting. One clear request with a named date gives them something to vote on."
- **Method:** `decisionMaker` "your school board", `request` "Start high school classes at 8:30 a.m. or later from next school year."
- **Lens and ambition:** `policy`, `this-year`.

Bad:
- **Title:** "Advocate for better sleep policies"

This one names no one who can say yes and asks for nothing specific. "Advocate" hides the request.
