Riso inks on Radix scales

Each Radix Colors scale is matched to the closest riso ink from the stencil.wiki ink list (80 inks, by OKLab distance to Radix step 9). The ink is then run through Radix’s own custom-palette generator, which builds all 12 steps around it in light and dark. Step 9 is the ink itself (outlined). Notes

Ink values are the screen approximations listed on stencil.wiki’s “Riso inks” category, taken from each swatch’s RGB. Lagoon’s listed hex (#2F6F65) disagrees with its swatch (#2F6165); the swatch is used. Black, White and Clear Medium are left out.

Riso has only neutral grays, so Radix’s tinted grays (mauve, slate, sage, olive, sand) collapse onto Gray or Light Gray. Their scales come out identical even where Radix differs slightly. Bronze and gold aren’t in the generator’s base set, so the generator builds them from neighbouring scales.

Generator: components/generate-radix-colors.tsx from radix-ui/website (MIT), with @radix-ui/colors 3.0.0, colorjs.io 0.5.2 and bezier-easing 2.1.0. Backgrounds: white #FFFFFF and #111111 (Radix defaults), cream #F7F3EA and #1A1815.
