# It MVP Onboarding Content Spec

Personal pains, personal dreams, world pains, world dreams, tiering,
copy rules, and implementation notes

## Core model

The onboarding has four independently curated surfaces: **Personal
Pains, Personal Dreams, World Pains, and World Dreams**. They relate
many-to-many; dreams are not mechanically generated as negations of
pains.

Tier 1 is the presentation surface. Everything else remains real and
addressable at Tier 2.

## Copy rules

-   Pain copy describes the present experience.
-   Dream copy describes a desirable state, not merely the absence of
    the pain.
-   Preserve candidate wording even when UI copy changes.
-   Tag world pains by causal role (condition / mechanism-cause / risk).
-   Large structural problems must connect first actions to credible
    larger pathways.

## Tier 1: Personal pains → dreams

### Body

-   **Poor sleep** --- I don't sleep well. → **Rested** --- I wake up
    ready.
-   **Out of shape** --- I don't move enough. → **Strong** --- my body
    can do what I ask.

### Head

-   **Anxiety** --- I worry too much. → **Calm** --- my head is quiet.
-   **Burnout** --- I've got nothing left. → **Recovered** --- I have
    something left at the end of the day.
-   **Overwhelmed** --- there's too much at once. → **On top of it** ---
    I know what today is for.

### Money

-   **Broke** --- money is always tight. → **Steady / Comfortable /
    Free** --- a bad month stays a bad month. / I don't think about
    money much. / I don't work just to live.
-   **Underpaid** --- my work is worth more. → **Paid fairly** --- what
    I get matches what I do.

### Time

-   **Doomscrolling** --- I lose my nights to my phone. → **Present**
    --- my evenings are mine.
-   **No free time** --- none of it is mine. → **Unhurried** --- there's
    room in the day.

### People

-   **Loneliness** --- I feel alone. → **Belonging** --- my people are
    close by.
-   **Nobody to do things with** --- I'd go if someone asked. → **A
    regular thing** --- there's something on the calendar with people in
    it.
-   **Drifted apart** --- I've lost touch with people who matter. →
    **Back in touch** --- I see them and it's easy again.

### Doing

-   **Procrastination** --- I put things off. → **Follow-through** --- I
    do what I said I'd do.
-   **No path** --- I know what I want and not how. → **The next step**
    --- the way there is obvious from here.
-   **Meaningless work** --- my work doesn't matter. → **Purpose** ---
    my work is worth doing.

## Tier 1: World pains → dreams

### Power

-   **They don't listen** --- nobody in charge answers to me. → **Real
    representation** --- my rep actually calls back.
-   **I don't know who to vote for** --- the ballot is a guess. →
    **Confident at the ballot** --- I vote knowing what I'm voting for.
-   **No accountability** --- they say anything and nothing follows. →
    **Watched** --- we know who kept their promises.

### Economy

-   **Rent eats everything** --- housing takes half. → **Affordable
    housing** --- rent leaves room for a life.
-   **One illness away** --- getting sick would ruin me. →
    **Healthcare** --- you get care when you need it.
-   **It all goes up** --- the people who own things get everything. →
    **Shared ownership** --- the profit stays here.

### Each other

-   **Nobody knows anybody** --- my street is strangers. → **Real
    community** --- there are people here who show up.
-   **We can't talk** --- disagreeing ends it. → **Common ground** ---
    we argue and still get things done.

### Dignity

-   **Treated differently** --- who I am counts against me. → **Taken as
    I am** --- I walk in and it's not a factor.
-   **People get treated badly for who they are** --- I watch it happen.
    → **Everyone's counted in** --- nobody has to shrink to be safe
    here.

### Information

-   **I don't know what's true** --- everything contradicts everything.
    → **Honest news** --- I trust what I read.
-   **Nobody covers here** --- I don't know what's happening in my own
    town. → **Local coverage** --- someone reports on this place.

### Change

-   **Nothing ever changes** --- we all know the problem and it stays. →
    **Momentum** --- things we agree on actually happen.
-   **Shouting into the void** --- I care and it goes nowhere. → **It
    counted** --- what I did showed up somewhere.
-   **Alone in caring** --- I don't know who else gives a damn. →
    **Found my people** --- there's a group and it's local.

## Design notes

-   "Nobody to do things with" and "Loneliness" both stay. One is a
    state; the other is a logistics complaint. The latter is unusually
    fixable and is valuable early in onboarding.
-   On the Tier 1 World screen, Dignity sits between Each other and
    Information deliberately. At the end it risks reading as an
    appendix; between ordinary domains it reads as ordinary.
-   "The turnaround" is not a pain-group item. It is retained as a world
    dream / possible standalone experiment because it describes a
    desired trajectory.
-   "Needed" and "Useful" should receive special protection during
    curation. They point outward and create a natural bridge from
    personal improvement to social participation.
-   The dream surface needs some expansive personal aspirations so Act 3
    has somewhere to climb. "Free," "Room to breathe," and "My own
    thing" are current candidates.
-   "Everyone's fed" is retained as a positive-state formulation. It is
    slightly less forceful than "no hunger," but it depicts an
    affirmative state and supports a concrete illustration.
-   Climate and AI require especially strong fix-side pathways. If the
    app answers them with a generic personal habit, the product promise
    collapses.

## Data / implementation model

Pains and dreams are independent nodes with many-to-many relations.
Recommended fields: stable ID; scope; domain; tier; node type; display
label; display copy; image concept; related pain/dream IDs; Tier 2
children; causal role; intervention scale; recommendation/action links.

## Illustration direction

Use the established black-ink woodcut/riso-like visual language: high
contrast, sparse composition, chunky imperfect shapes, hand-printed
grain, generous whitespace, and one immediately legible metaphor per
card.
