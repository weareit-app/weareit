# 2026-10-30

Proposed, not decided: protect any count of people according to what it counts, not with one rule for every count. Public preference (for example "I want this fix in my neighborhood") is shown exact, with no account stored next to the count; personal circumstance (childcare, transport, work, money trouble) gets coarser place and time and a minimum count below which it is not shown; sensitive counts are not shown for small groups. Default sensitive fix categories, whose counts are coarsened rather than exact even though choosing a fix is a preference, proposed for the founder to edit: immigration and legal-status services; clinic access, including reproductive and sexual health; addiction and recovery support; mental health care; domestic violence and abuse support; disability services; debt and eviction help; and reentry after prison. Why: in a small place, a count of the people who want a sensitive service can point at individuals, while adding noise or suppression to every count would make the ordinary small counts at launch unreadable. Scope today: v0.1 shows no count of people on pain pages (spec 012), and founder-only admin has no small-number suppression (2026-10-19), so this record applies to any count shown beyond founder-only admin and asks whether sensitive categories should be coarsened in admin views too. No code changes with this record.
