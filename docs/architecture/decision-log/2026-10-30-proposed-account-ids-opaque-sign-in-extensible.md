# 2026-10-30

Proposed, not decided: keep the account model open to sign-in methods beyond email without adding any now. `user.id` stays an opaque random string that is never shown or used as a public handle or in URLs; the auth boundary stays in one place (`createAuth` and `getSession`), as passkeys already do; other tables keep referencing `user.id`, not email; and any later credential (another email, a device key) is linked to the existing account rather than replacing it. Why: every user-owned table cascades from `user.id`. If the email or the ID becomes a public identifier, changing how people sign in, merging duplicate accounts or letting someone change their email becomes a migration across every table. Alternatives: use email as the user-facing handle (simpler now, costly to change); do nothing explicit. No code changes with this record.
