## 1. The question
- People want different lives. Can a society get better for most of them without agreeing on one picture of a good life?

## 2. What is known
- Development as expanding what people can actually do and be, rather than one measure of income (Sen).
- Limits on turning many people's preferences into one group choice (Arrow), and Sen's argument that richer information makes social choice possible after all.
- Designs for more people to take part in deciding: citizens' assemblies and lottery-selected bodies (Landemore). Proposals for institutions that already exist in small form (Wright's "real utopias").
- What people say bothers them: It's own societal pains registry (`docs/source/MVP_Societal_Pains_Source_Registry.md`) and its sources.
