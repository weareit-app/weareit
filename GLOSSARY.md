# It

We Are It helps people see that a better personal and shared future is possible, find a concrete path toward it, and make progress with others.

## Language

**We Are It**:
The full name of both the organization and the app.
_Avoid_: weareit, WeAreIt

**It**:
The short name for We Are It, the organization or the app.
_Avoid_: the It app, it (lowercase)

### Pains, dreams, and fixes

**Pain**:
Something wrong in a person's life or in society, named in the first person.

**Dream**:
A desired end state for a pain: what it's like when that pain is resolved, said in the first person. It is not a means to something else within the pain. It may also be the end state of neighboring pains, but it still names this pain's domain, and it is more specific than a general value such as being happy.
_Avoid_: future, possible future, goal, outcome

**Means**:
A specific way of reaching a dream, such as a program, product, service, organization, or single action. A means is written as a fix, never as a dream.
_Avoid_: solution

**Fix**:
One concrete thing that could help reach a dream, labelled with its kind and its honest status.
_Avoid_: way, solution

**Fix kind**:
The form a fix takes, such as a feature, task, habit, or group. Each fix has exactly one kind.
_Avoid_: fix type, type
