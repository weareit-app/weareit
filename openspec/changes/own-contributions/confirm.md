# own-contributions: questions for the founder

Answer each by number. "yes" accepts the recommendation. The specs are written to the recommendations; any other answer changes the requirement named.

1. **Unsent contributions after signup.** The signup save sends only picks, selected fixes and the opt-in, and the decision says an own item is sent only when the person chooses "Send for review". So where do unsent ones live after signup?
   - A. They stay on this device only (not sent, not cleared by the signup save, not on other devices) until sent or removed.
   - B. The signup gate offers to keep them privately in the account (adds an item to the gate's "what will be saved" list).
   - C. They are cleared at signup unless sent first.
   - Recommendation: A. Affects on-device-choices "Own contributions survive the signup save".

2. **Sign-out on a shared device.** Unsent own items are on the device, not in the account.
   - A. Remove them on sign-out, with a warning that unsent items will be lost.
   - B. Keep them visible to whoever uses the device next.
   - C. Keep them tied to that account on this device and show them only when it signs in again (items made before signup are tied to the account at signup).
   - Recommendation: C.

3. **Sending a child of your own item** (a future under your own pain, or a way under your own future).
   - A. Sending it also sends its unsent own parents, after saying so; the reviewer sees them together.
   - B. It can't be sent until its parent has been added.
   - C. Each is sent and reviewed on its own.
   - Recommendation: A.

4. **Changing your mind while "In review".**
   - A. The person can withdraw it (it leaves the queue and goes back to "Only you can see this"); no editing while in review.
   - B. Editing while in review updates the queued item.
   - C. Neither; once sent it is the founder's to decide.
   - Recommendation: A.

5. **A way's line.** FR-021 says every contribution has a name and a first-person line, but published ways have plain descriptions ("Paired with a person working on the same thing.").
   - A. Ways get a name and a plain one-line description, like published ways.
   - B. Ways get a first-person line too (FR-021 as written).
   - Recommendation: A. Affects own-contributions "Add a way" wording only.

6. **Way types a contributor can choose.**
   - A. Task, Habit or Takes a group; only the reviewer can make a way a Feature.
   - B. All four; an own Feature shows the "Idea" status.
   - Recommendation: A, so a person's idea is never shown as something It might build without review.

7. **How strict the first-person check is.** Some published lines start "Someone", "The" or "What".
   - A. One sentence, starting with a capital, ending in a single period, no "?" or "!", and containing a first-person word (I, I'm, me, my, mine, we, us, our).
   - B. Must start with "I", "I'm", "My" or "We".
   - C. Only "one sentence ending in a period".
   - Recommendation: A, and show it as a warning (not a block) on founder-written futures in the editor.

8. **When "Added" shows.**
   - A. Only once the approved item is in a published version (it is actually visible to others).
   - B. As soon as the founder approves it.
   - Recommendation: A. Affects contribution-review "Approve with edits".

9. **Wording after an edited approval.**
   - A. The contributor's page shows the published record (founder's wording), with their pick moved to it.
   - B. The contributor keeps seeing their own wording.
   - Recommendation: A.

10. **Length limits.**
   - A. Name up to 60 characters, line up to 160 characters.
   - B. Other numbers (say which).
   - Recommendation: A, as placeholders the founder can change.

11. **Limits and crisis guidance** (decisions.md marks both VERIFY before launch).
   - A. Launch with a placeholder of 20 sends per account per day and a short reviewer note on what to do when a contribution suggests someone is in crisis, both written or approved by the founder before launch.
   - B. Decide the numbers and guidance now (say what).
   - Recommendation: A; launch blocks until the founder approves both.

12. **Account deletion.**
   - A. Published contributions stay as ordinary content; their provenance is removed; anything in review or declined is deleted.
   - B. Keep provenance after deletion for abuse handling.
   - Recommendation: A.

13. **Pain pages.** Should "Add a future" and "Add a way" also appear on a pain's own page at `/p/<slug>`?
   - A. Yes, the same as on the personal and societal pages.
   - B. No, only on the personal and societal pages.
   - Recommendation: A.

14. **Who the reviewer sees.**
   - A. The queue hides who sent an item by default; the founder can reveal it for abuse handling.
   - B. Always shown.
   - Recommendation: A.
