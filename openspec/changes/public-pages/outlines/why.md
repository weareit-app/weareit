# /why: What made this worth attempting?

**Writer:** founder, first person. **Target:** about 500 words, three dated milestones, one link into the explorer.

**Guardrail (atlas):** trust comes from checkable work and candor, not from a credentials-first bio or from how long you've been at it.

## 1. The question (1 paragraph)

Why build It at all, when there are already apps for habits, causes, petitions and self-help?

- **You:** the moment or pattern that made you think the way we currently approach our lives and shared problems could be better. A specific scene beats a summary.

## 2. What you noticed (2 paragraphs)

The gap It tries to fill: people can usually name what hurts, but rarely see the several different futures that could follow, or the concrete things that would move them toward one, alone or with others.

- **You:** one example from your own life where seeing more than one possible future changed what you did.
- **You:** why the existing options (products, institutions, movements you've tried) didn't close that gap for you. Be fair to them.

## 3. What It is trying to change (1 paragraph)

From one person's pain → several better futures → ways to get there, and later, finding others who want the same future. Say plainly that today It is the explorer plus signup, and the rest is the roadmap (link `/roadmap`).

## 4. How we got here (timeline block)

Three or four dated turning points, each with something checkable behind it.

- **You:** pick the milestones. Candidates from the repo: the first It spec and source documents (October 2026), the brand and the watercolor mark (2026-10-18), the first working explorer.
- **You:** anything earlier (research, past projects) you're willing to date and link.

## 5. One thing that changed your mind, one you still can't answer (1 paragraph)

The atlas asks for both. This is what makes the page honest rather than heroic.

- **You:** a belief you held at the start that the work changed.
- **You:** the open question that worries you most about whether this can work.

## 6. Who is building this (short)

It is one person right now ("the 'we' in 'We Are It' is just me, and hopefully you, too", from the Support letter). Operated by We Are It LLC. No venture capital, no ads, no selling data.

- Name yourself by first name, Josh (founder, 2026-10-19). **You:** how much of your situation to share. The UX spec notes your financial constraints are internal background, not public copy, unless you choose otherwise.
