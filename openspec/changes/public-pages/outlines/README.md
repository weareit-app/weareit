# Essay outlines

Starting points for the five v0.1 essays, so nobody writes from a blank page. They come from the Website Page Atlas v0.2 (`docs/source/We_Are_It_Website_Page_Atlas_v0.2_2026-10-16.md`, §2 and §8) and the repo's current decisions.

- **Founder writes**: `why.md`, `ai.md`. Questions marked **You:** are things only you can answer; the rest is structure and reminders.
- **Drafted, then founder rewrites**: `principles.md`, `risks.md`, `roadmap.md`. These have enough detail for a first draft.

Every essay follows the atlas's six-part shape loosely: the question, what is known, our present view (labelled), why it matters, what remains open, and where to go next. About 500 words is enough to publish a first version. Voice: talk to one person as an adult, plainly and calmly, with specifics and no hype words or exclamation marks (the draft copy style guide on the `codex/possibilities-pilot` branch, `docs/style/copy-style-guide.md`).

Each outline ends with **Blocks** (the named blocks the essay could use, at most one or two to start) and **Sources to find**. Every figure needs a real source before publishing; none are filled in here.
