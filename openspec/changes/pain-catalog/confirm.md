# pain-catalog: founder answers

Grilled 2026-10-19, with draft spec 004 (pilot repo) as evidence. Spec 004's own answers still hold: onboarding pains are one-line pains a person recognizes as their own, and narrower child pains may sit under a `broader` relation (its Q1). Its "packages/content for now" home (Q2) is replaced by 1 below. Post-signup aggregate input (Q3) is deferred by 4.

1. **Where the catalog lives.** The catalog is a Git research catalog at `docs/research/pains/catalog.yaml`, seeded from the 125-pain inventory, holding candidates, rejected items, coverage and history. Once a pain is accepted into the content editor, the editor owns its wording. A drift check flags differences and never overwrites. **Founder: yes.** (design.md decision 1 keeps the place and role but stores the file as `catalog.json`, so the stdlib tools can read it.)
2. **Merges.** A merge retires the old pain with the surviving pain as its replacement (pain-pages mechanism). The catalog still records it as a merge. **Founder: yes.**
3. **Review page.** A static page generated by the tool, like the audit and calibration pages. Decisions are exported as JSON for ingest. It is separate from the in-app contributions review queue. Choosing a decision never moves on by itself. **Founder: yes.**
4. **Post-signup themes.** Out of this change; room is left for them. **Founder: yes.**
5. **Tool shape.** `tools/pains/` (stdlib Python with tests) and the skills `/pains-discover`, `/pains-refine`, `/pains-review` and `/pains-ingest`. The stale-run check lives in `tools/possibilities`. **Founder: yes.**
6. **Founder's pain pool.** Copy both v2 CSVs into `docs/research/pains/sources/`, marked as AI-generated leads, after checking they hold nothing about the founder personally. **Founder: yes.**
7. **First run.** Pilot discover and refine on Money and Economy, then the founder reviews, then a full sweep. **Founder: yes.**
8. **Roles.** "More like this" no longer exists. A pain is either **shown** (published under its heading) or **not shown** (kept in the catalog only). The catalog can hold many pains beyond the curated, shown ones. **Founder (own words).**
9. **What ingest writes.** Accepted wording and group changes to pains already in the editor go into its draft right away. A new shown pain goes in only once it has founder-approved futures. Ingest never publishes. **Founder: yes.**
10. **Sensitive pains.** Pains are tagged as sensitive. Lint flags graphic wording for the founder to look at, but never fails it or excludes the pain because of it ("We're not here to deny reality"). Showing crisis resources first is out of scope. **Founder (own words).**
11. **Sources and target.** Use 211's public top-needs reports instead of the paid AIRS taxonomy. Keep the rest of the list, and stay open to any other good source. Target: 90% coverage of each outside list after the first full sweep, with a reason for every uncovered entry. **Founder: yes, plus "be open to sourcing from elsewhere".**
12. **Two review steps.** Wording first (new pains, rewordings), then overlaps and group moves, built after the wording is ingested so they show the final wording. **Founder (own words), 2026-10-20.**
13. **Placement waits for ranking.** Review doesn't decide whether a new pain is shown. Every accepted new pain waits for ranking, with no separate "catalog only" choice, and body-mind's 16 new pains wait too. **Founder, 2026-10-20.**
