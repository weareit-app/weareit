## 7. Privacy and safety (every page)

Privacy page — "What stays with you, and what we count"

- ✔ **core** One line on every page before signup: your picks stay on this device; we count only how far visits get. Links to the privacy page.
- ✔ **core** Privacy page in plain words: what stays on your device, what we count before signup (coarse totals and which stages a visit reached, never your picks), what is saved when you sign up or support, who handles it, nothing public by default.
- ✔ **core** Works without an account and without a connection.
- ? **core** Delete your account (self-serve or on request).
- ? *(optional)* Separate legal privacy policy.

Safety floor — "Need help now?" (? CANDIDATE, founder to confirm)

- ? **core** Free 24/7 crisis lines for your region, tap to call or text, plus a link to find a helpline anywhere. Works offline.
- ? **core** Plain notice: no one at It is watching what you write or pick, and It can't respond in an emergency.
- ? *(optional)* Gentle prompt with crisis lines when something you type suggests you're struggling. Checked on your device only; nothing is sent.
- ? *(optional)* Report an idea that's wrong or harmful, or a privacy problem. Shows exactly what will be sent before you send it.
