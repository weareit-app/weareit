# Problem Definition: It MVP (v0.1)

- **Slug**: it-mvp
- **Created**: 2026-10-16
- **Inputs used**: intake.md, research.md, founder answers (2026-10-16)

## Problem Statement

People feel everyday personal and societal pains, but often can't put a better future into words or see a believable first step, and tools that ask them to write about themselves add stress rather than help. At the same time It, a one-person project under near-term financial pressure, has no evidence that people value this and no income, so it needs to find out soon whether the public will support it.

## Affected Users & Stakeholders

- **Users — members of the public with a pain they'd like to change** (general public at launch; about 100–1,000 people expected in month one). They lose time and hope to problems they can't see past. One informal tester found picking from options intuitive and less stressful than writing about herself. — [research: Users & Demand; founder answers]
- **Users — people who care about societal problems** but feel nothing changes or that they are alone in caring. — [research: Tier 1 world pains, draft; low confidence]
- **Stakeholder — the founder.** Decides scope, writes or approves all content, carries the cost, and needs income soon. — [research: Market & Context]
- **Stakeholder — future supporters and helpers.** Fund or build It. They need honest information about what is real today and what is only planned. — [research: Data & Constraints, content honesty]

## Goals

1. **Primary (founder's choice): people choose to support It** with money (once, monthly or yearly) after seeing what it offers.
2. People find at least one better future they hadn't put into words and an idea they would actually use, before anyone asks them for anything. This is what earns support. — [research: draft UX spec §13.1]
3. Visitors trust It: they can tell what exists now from what is proposed, and never feel pushed or used. — [AGENTS.md; research]
4. People who explore choose to stay in touch (sign up), creating a first group of people for It to build with.
5. The founder learns quickly what works, without collecting personal picks before signup. — [privacy invariants]

## Non-Goals

- Native iOS release or iOS payments (web only for v0.1; founder 2026-10-16).
- Personal story, sharing, invitations, matching people, starting from a dream (all "soon" or optional).
- Proving that ideas change people's lives; v0.1 only tests whether they seem useful.
- Charity status, tax-deductible claims or "donation" wording.
- Any tracking of individual picks before signup, or stable anonymous IDs.
- Systematic user research before launch (founder chose launch, then learn).

## Success Metrics

No numeric targets: the founder continues regardless, so these metrics show what to change next. Baselines are all zero or unknown because nothing has launched.

- **Supporters:** share of visitors who complete a support payment, plus count and recurring vs. one-time. (baseline: 0) Research suggests voluntary support converts around 1%, which means roughly 1–10 supporters from 100–1,000 visitors.
- **Support page reach:** share of signed-up people who open the Support page, and share of those who pay. (baseline: unknown)
- **Usefulness:** share of visitors who pick at least one dream, and the number of "I'd use this" marks per visitor. (baseline: unknown)
- **Signup gate:** share of people finishing both pages who sign up. Review line: if more than about half leave at signup, revisit required signup (founder 2026-10-16). (baseline: unknown)
- **Funnel:** stage-by-stage completion (open pain → pick dream → see idea → societal page → signup → Support → Help). (baseline: unknown)
- **Trust (qualitative):** replies or messages saying people felt pressured or misled; target is none. Honesty-label errors found after launch; target is zero.

## Cost of Inaction

The founder keeps carrying the cost with no income and no evidence. The financial pressure is real and near-term, and every week without a launch is a week without learning whether people value It or will pay for it. The ideas, content and scaffold already built stay unused. People get nothing new: personal pains stay with self-help apps and societal ones with petitions and news, with nothing that joins them or shows a path.
